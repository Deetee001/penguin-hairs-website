Why Our Hair Is Different

Understanding quality makes all the difference

100% Raw Donor Hair

Our raw donor hair is unprocessed and comes from a single donor, ensuring all cuticles are intact and aligned. This means minimal tangling, natural shine, and hair that can be colored and styled just like your own.

Double Drawn Quality

Double drawn hair means shorter hairs have been removed, leaving only the longest strands. This creates uniform thickness from root to tip, giving you fuller, more voluminous hair throughout the entire length.
